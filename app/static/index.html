How this works

Start with the market

Completed daily OHLCV candles come from Yahoo Finance via yfinance. Stock prices are adjusted. The last observation date is always shown.

Let Kronos find a path

The open-source model uses up to 256 candles of context. Three sampled paths are averaged into the closing prices you see. Results can vary between runs.

Keep the uncertainty

A forecast is an estimate, not a guarantee or a confidence interval. Use this as a research tool, alongside your own judgment.
